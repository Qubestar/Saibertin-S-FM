M4C expands the command-center layer to 64 logical sequencer/MIDI tracks: banks A–D, 16 tracks each. Every track keeps its own length, MIDI channel, steps, CCs and locks. The FM-1/SLOOP audio engine still has four native internal parts, so A01–A04 may use INTERNAL/BOTH while the remaining command-center tracks are MIDI-only. Multiple USB virtual MIDI buses are a later firmware-routing stage, not assumed yet.
